OFFICIAL COLLEGE ADMISSION GUIDE - 2026/2027

The official name of this college is Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences, widely known as AITS. 

1. INTRODUCTION & ADMISSION POLICIES
The college, Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences (AITS), is committed to admitting students who show high academic potential and alignment with our values of innovation and leadership. Admissions at AITS are conducted on a rolling basis for the Fall and Spring semesters. 

- Admission Policy: Admission to Apex Institute of Technology & Sciences (AITS) is open to all qualified students regardless of race, gender, religion, or nationality. 
- Application Policy: Students can submit only one application per academic session to AITS. If rejected by the college, they must wait until the next term to reapply.

2. COURSES OFFERED & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

AITS offers the following undergraduate programs:

A. Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science (B.Tech CS)
- Eligibility: Minimum 75% or equivalent GPA in High School (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as compulsory subjects.
- Prerequisite: Satisfactory score in the National Science Entrance Exam or SAT.

B. Bachelor of Science in Data Science (B.Sc Data Science)
- Eligibility: Minimum 70% in High School with Mathematics as a compulsory subject.
- Prerequisite: High School completion certificate.

C. Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
- Eligibility: Minimum 60% in High School in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts).
- Prerequisite: Good communication skills and clearance of the internal aptitude test.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

Q1: What is the refund policy for tuition fees if I withdraw my admission?
A: If you withdraw your admission before the semester classes begin, a 90% refund of the tuition fee will be processed. If you withdraw within the first 14 days of classes, a 50% refund is issued. No refunds will be provided after 14 days of classes starting.

Q2: Does AITS offer scholarships?
A: Yes! AITS offers Merit-based scholarships covering up to 50% of the tuition fee for students scoring above 95% in high school. Financial need-based scholarships are also available up to 30% of the tuition fee.

Q3: Can I transfer credits from another university?
A: Yes, AITS accepts credit transfers from accredited universities. At least 60% of the course content must match AITS curriculum, and the transfer student must maintain a minimum GPA of 3.0.

Q4: Are international students required to submit English proficiency scores?
A: Yes. International applicants whose native language is not English must submit IELTS (minimum band score of 6.5) or TOEFL (minimum score of 80) test results.

Q5: Is there an application fee?
A: Yes, there is a non-refundable application fee of $50 for domestic applicants and $75 for international applicants.
